Frequently Asked Questions

My attendance CSV will not upload. What should I do?

Make sure you are uploading a CSV file downloaded from Zoom. The file should include Name (Original Name), User Email, Join Time, and Duration (Minutes) columns.

What session date should I select?

Select the date of the Zoom session you want to record attendance for. The app will only use attendance records from that date.

How is attendance calculated?

Attendance is calculated using the total Duration (Minutes) recorded for each Fellow. Fellows who attended for at least 80 minutes are marked Present. Fellows who attended for less than 80 minutes are marked Absent.

What happens if someone joins Zoom more than once?

The app adds together their attendance duration from multiple Zoom sessions on the selected date.

Where are the results saved?

Results are added to the Program Roster Google Sheet in a new attendance tab for the selected session date.

What if a Fellow does not appear in the Zoom attendance report?

The Fellow will be marked Absent for that session.
